# Product: GhostStack

A modern revival of Apple's 1987 HyperCard for small business owners (shop
owners, market traders, farm-produce sellers). Users build their own simple
business tool by arranging "cards" and "buttons" in a "stack", with no coding.

## Users

Non-technical owners of small shops and stalls. They use smartphones, have
patchy internet, and currently track money in notebooks.

## Core promise

Build a working stock, sales, and debt tracker in under 5 minutes. Works fully
offline. The numbers are always correct.

## MVP scope (do not exceed)

- 4 card types: Stock, Sales log, Debts, Daily summary
- Fixed button actions: add item, record sale, record debt, mark debt paid,
  show today's totals, go to card
- Builder mode (add, rename, reorder, delete cards) and Play mode (everyday use)
- 2 or 3 starter stacks
- Data saved on the device; installable as a PWA

## Out of scope

Accounts, cloud sync, payments, bank integration, multi-user, a scripting
language, native apps.

## Hackathon context

Kiroween 2026, Resurrection category. Judged on impact, Kiro usage, design,
innovation, and presentation.
